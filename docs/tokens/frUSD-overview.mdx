# frUSD Overview
## The Dollar, Settled on Bitcoin L1

`frUSD` is a US-dollar token that lives on Bitcoin L1 as an [Alkanes](../protocol/alkanes) asset. It holds an ordinary Bitcoin UTXO, moves in an ordinary Bitcoin transaction, and can be composed into the same contract calls as any other Alkanes token. That is what separates it from a dollar that merely *references* Bitcoin from another chain.

It is live on mainnet at alkane id `4:1776`, with **8 decimals**.

The point of frUSD is a BTC/USD market that clears on proof-of-work. The dollars most of the world actually uses settle somewhere else and ask Bitcoin to wait; frUSD settles where the bitcoin already is.

## Where the dollars are

frUSD is **fully reserved, not algorithmic**. There is no on-chain collateral ratio to watch, no liquidation engine, and no seigniorage. Each frUSD exists because someone deposited a dollar-denominated stablecoin on Ethereum:

1. A user deposits **USDC or USDT** into the SUBFROST bridge vault on Ethereum.
2. The SUBFROST coordinator observes the deposit and mints the matching `frUSD` on Bitcoin, to the Bitcoin address named in the deposit.
3. Burning `frUSD` on Bitcoin is the reverse instruction: it releases value back out on Ethereum.

Anchoring to USDC and USDT is a deliberate choice rather than a shortcut. On-chain, the canonical dollar is already settled: USDC and USDT are what the market quotes against and treats as cash. frUSD does not try to unseat that, it anchors to it, so the peg tracks the dollar the ecosystem has already agreed on instead of a synthetic approximation. What SUBFROST changes is not the dollar itself but the layer you hold and move it on.

The deposited stablecoins are not left idle. They are deployed by the protocol operator into [Curve's 3pool](https://curve.fi/), so the reserve backing frUSD is held as 3CRV liquidity rather than as raw USDC and USDT.

<Admonition type="warning" title="The trust model, stated plainly">

frUSD does **not** inherit frBTC's custody story, and the two should not be read as equally trust-minimized.

`frBTC` is secured by a [FROST & ROAST](../key-components/frost-and-roast) threshold-signature group whose decentralization roadmap is public. `frUSD` is different by construction: **its reserve lives on Ethereum, in a vault the SUBFROST operator controls**, and minting is mediated by an operator-run coordinator. The bridge vault and the `4:1776` token are both upgradeable proxies, and parameters such as the minimum bridge amount are owner-mutable.

That is the honest description of the system as built today. It has not been through a formal security audit. Use it with that in mind.

</Admonition>

## How can I get frUSD?

### 1. Swap for it in the frUSD/frBTC pool

The most direct route, and the one that never leaves Bitcoin. `frUSD` trades against `frBTC` in a [CryptoSwap pool](../using-subfrost/pools-liquidity) at `4:1778`, so BTC-denominated value can be moved into dollars and back without touching another chain.

### 2. Mint it by bridging USDC or USDT from Ethereum

You deposit on Ethereum and the `frUSD` is minted to a Bitcoin address you name. Minting is subject to a fee on the Ethereum side, and burning back out has a **minimum of 10 frUSD** (an owner-mutable parameter, read from the chain rather than hardcoded).

In the app, choose USDC or USDT as what you are paying with and `frUSD` as what you are receiving. You will be asked to connect an Ethereum wallet and sign two transactions: an approval for exactly the amount you are depositing, and the deposit itself.

If you would rather drive it yourself, [BTC/USD Commands](../api-reference/cli-sdk/btcusd) prints the same two calls for you to sign in your own wallet.

:::danger[A plain transfer to the vault is not a deposit]
The deposit must be made through the bridge contract's own deposit call, which carries the instruction naming your Bitcoin address. **Sending USDC or USDT to the vault address as an ordinary token transfer does not mint anything**: it arrives with no instruction attached, and there is no automatic route back.
:::

### 3. Receive it

`frUSD` is an ordinary Alkanes token. Any wallet that handles Alkanes assets can hold, send, and receive it, and it shows up alongside your other balances.

## What is live today

Being specific about status matters, so here is where each piece stands.

| | Status |
|---|---|
| The `frUSD` token on Bitcoin mainnet (`4:1776`) | **Live** |
| Minting by bridging USDC/USDT from Ethereum | **Live**, in the app or from the command line |
| Burning frUSD back out to Ethereum | **Live** |
| Splitting part of a deposit into BTC on the way in | **Live**. A split the network cannot execute pays plain frUSD instead, so read the notes on [BTC/USD Commands](../api-reference/cli-sdk/btcusd) before using it |
