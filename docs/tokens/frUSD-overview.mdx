# frUSD - Ultimate Stablecoin

:::info[Live on mainnet]
frUSD, the Bitcoin-native stablecoin, is live. It trades against frBTC in a pool on mainnet, and it can be minted by bridging USDC or USDT in from Ethereum. A full page describing the design will land here; until then the pages below cover what you can do with it today.
:::

- [Swap](../using-subfrost/swap): trade BTC against USD through the frUSD/frBTC pool.
- [Pools & Liquidity](../using-subfrost/pools-liquidity): what providing liquidity to that pool pays, and how its fee differs from a standard AMM pool.
- [BTC/USD Commands](../api-reference/cli-sdk/btcusd): read the pool, quote a trade, or prepare a bridge deposit from the command line.
