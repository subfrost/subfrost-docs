# dxBTC Minting and Burning

:::info Planned
dxBTC is planned and not live. Minting and burning `dxBTC` are not available yet, and there is no public command for them today. The `subfrost-cli` commands this page used to describe are retired and not available.
:::

## Intended Flow

### Minting `dxBTC`

Once dxBTC launches, a deposit will first become `frBTC`. Minting will then lock up that `frBTC` in the dxBTC alkane and mint `dxBTC` to your Bitcoin address.

### Burning `dxBTC`

Burning will burn your `dxBTC` and unlock `frBTC` to your Bitcoin address, which you will then be able to unwrap to BTC.
